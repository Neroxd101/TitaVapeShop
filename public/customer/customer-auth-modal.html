• Ensuring strict compliance with minimum age restrictions.

Your data is encrypted in transit and securely held. Passwords are cryptographically hashed. We will never sell, lease, or distribute your personal details to third parties.

You have the legal right to be informed, access, correct, object, or request blocking/erasure of your stored personal data at any time.
